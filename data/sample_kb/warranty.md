# Warranty Policy — NovaGadget

## Standard warranty
All NovaGadget devices come with a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage, or normal wear and tear.

## Extended warranty
Customers can purchase NovaGadget Care+ at checkout or within 30 days of purchase, extending coverage to 3 years and adding one accidental-damage repair per year for a $25 deductible.

## Filing a warranty claim
Warranty claims require the order number or serial number, and a description of the issue. Support may ask for a photo or short video of the defect. Approved claims receive a prepaid shipping label to send the device in for inspection.

## Repair vs replacement
NovaGadget decides whether to repair or replace a defective unit based on the nature of the fault and part availability. Replacement units may be refurbished but are functionally equivalent to new.

## Out-of-warranty repairs
Devices outside the warranty period can still be repaired for a flat fee depending on the model, quoted after inspection. Customers are not charged unless they approve the quote.
