# Shipping Policy — NovaGadget

## Domestic shipping
Orders placed before 2 PM ET ship the same business day. Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days and costs $4.99, or is free on orders over $50. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs $14.99.

## International shipping
We ship to Canada, the UK, and the EU. International orders take 7-14 business days and cost a flat $19.99. Customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes charged by their country.

## Order tracking
Every order gets a tracking number by email within 24 hours of shipment. Customers can also check order status in the "My Orders" section of their account, or by giving support their order number.

## Delayed or lost packages
If a package hasn't moved in tracking for more than 5 business days (domestic) or 14 business days (international), open a support ticket and we will investigate with the carrier. If a package is confirmed lost, we replace it or refund it at no cost to the customer.

## Address changes
Address changes are only possible if the order has not yet shipped. Once an order ships, we cannot redirect it — the customer must contact the carrier directly or wait for delivery and arrange a return.
