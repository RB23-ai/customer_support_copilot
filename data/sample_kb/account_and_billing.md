# Account and Billing FAQ — NovaGadget

## Updating payment methods
Customers can update saved payment methods under Account Settings > Payment Methods. Changes apply to future orders only; in-progress orders keep the original payment method.

## Subscription billing
NovaGadget Care+ and any subscription add-ons bill automatically each month on the signup anniversary date. Customers can cancel anytime from Account Settings > Subscriptions; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, with no partial refund for the remaining days.

## Failed payments
If a subscription payment fails, we retry automatically after 3 days and 7 days. After a second failed retry, the subscription is paused and the customer is notified by email to update their payment method.

## Duplicate charges
Duplicate charges are rare but can happen if a payment is retried during a network error. Confirmed duplicate charges are refunded automatically within 3 business days once verified by support; this verification requires a human agent.

## Closing an account
Account closure requests are handled by human support only, since it involves permanently deleting order history and personal data. The bot should always escalate account closure requests.
